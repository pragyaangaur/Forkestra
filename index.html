How it plays

  • Every day of the last year becomes one note, read left to right the way you read the graph itself.
  • The more commits a day holds, the higher and louder it rings. Your biggest days get answered by a second instrument.
  • Each week is carried by the cello underneath, and the whole chord turns over every four weeks.
  • Streaks call in the percussion, starting with the timpani and building through side drum and tambourine.
  • A light year opens out into something slower and more exposed, with the harp filling the space between your days and the whole piece lifted to carry.
  • Click anywhere on the graph to jump to that day. Space bar plays and pauses.

Only public contribution data is used. Nothing is stored and no sign in is needed.
